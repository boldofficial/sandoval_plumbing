## Plumbing Emergencies Don't Wait — And Neither Should You

In 25 years of plumbing in Chicago, I've seen it all. Burst pipes at 2 AM on Christmas morning. Sewage backups during dinner parties. Gas leaks on Super Bowl Sunday.

The most important thing is knowing **what to do** — and what NOT to do.

## Step 1: Shut Off the Water

Every member of your household should know where the **main water shut-off valve** is located. In most Chicago homes, it's in the basement or crawl space near the front of the house.

- **For a burst pipe or major leak:** Turn off the main water valve immediately.
- **For a toilet overflow:** Turn the shut-off valve behind the toilet clockwise.
- **For a sink leak:** Turn the shut-off valves under the sink.

**Pro tip:** If the valve hasn't been turned in years, it may be stuck. Call us immediately if you can't shut it off.

## Step 2: Shut Off the Water Heater

If you've shut off the main water supply, **turn off your water heater** to prevent damage:

- **Gas water heater:** Turn the thermostat to "Pilot" or "Off."
- **Electric water heater:** Turn off the breaker in your electrical panel.

Running a water heater without water will destroy the heating elements or cause the tank to overheat.

## Step 3: Address Gas Emergencies Immediately

If you smell natural gas (rotten egg odor) or hear a hissing sound:

1. **Do NOT** turn any lights on or off
2. **Do NOT** use any electrical switches or appliances
3. **Do NOT** use your phone inside the building
4. **Leave immediately** and call 911 from outside
5. Then call the gas company and Sandoval Plumbing

Gas leaks are life-threatening. Never attempt to fix a gas leak yourself.

## Step 4: Contain the Damage

After shutting off the water:

- Open faucets to drain remaining water from pipes
- Mop up standing water to prevent floor and wall damage
- Move furniture and valuables away from the affected area
- Place towels or buckets under active drips
- Document damage with photos for insurance purposes

## Common Chicago Plumbing Emergencies

### Burst Pipes (Winter)

The most common emergency. Pipes freeze and burst when temps drop below 15°F. Shut off water, call us immediately.

### Sewage Backup

If wastewater is backing up into your drains, stop using all water immediately. Sewage is a health hazard.

### Water Heater Failure

No hot water in a Chicago winter isn't just inconvenient — it's dangerous. We install replacement units same-day when possible.

### Gas Leak

Evacuate the building and call 911. We handle emergency gas line repairs.
