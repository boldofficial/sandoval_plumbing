## Gas Line Services in Chicago

Gas line work requires a licensed, insured professional. Sandoval Plumbing is fully licensed for gas line installation, repair, and bonding in Chicago. Never attempt gas line work yourself — it's dangerous and illegal without proper licensing.

### Signs of Gas Line Problems

- Smell of natural gas (rotten egg odor)
- Hissing sound near gas lines
- Dead vegetation near gas pipe routes
- Higher gas bills without increased usage
- Carbon monoxide detector alerts
- Gas appliances not working properly

### Our Gas Services

**Gas Line Installation:** New gas line installation for stoves, dryers, furnaces, water heaters, and outdoor grills.

**Gas Line Repair:** Emergency repair of damaged, leaking, or corroded gas lines.

**Gas Bonding:** Proper bonding of gas lines to prevent electrical hazards — required by Chicago code.

**Appliance Hookup:** Connecting and testing gas appliances for safe operation.

### Safety First

Gas leaks are dangerous and require immediate attention. If you smell gas, leave your home immediately and call 911. Then call Sandoval Plumbing at (773) 610-3344 for emergency gas line service.
