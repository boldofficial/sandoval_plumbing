## Drain Cleaning Services in Chicago

Slow or clogged drains are more than an inconvenience — they can lead to backups, water damage, and health hazards. Sandoval Plumbing offers comprehensive drain cleaning for residential and commercial properties.

### Signs You Need Drain Cleaning

- Water draining slowly in sinks or tubs
- Gurgling sounds from drains
- Foul odors coming from drains
- Water backing up when you run appliances
- Multiple drains clogged at once

### Our Drain Cleaning Methods

**Hydro-Jetting:** High-pressure water streams (up to 4,000 PSI) scour the inside of your pipes, removing grease, scale, and tree root intrusion. Ideal for kitchen drains and main sewer lines.

**Motorized Rodding:** For tough clogs, we use professional-grade augers and snakes to break through blockages. Effective for toilet clogs and local line stoppages.

**Camera Inspection:** We inspect your drains with a professional sewer camera to identify the exact cause and location of the problem — no guesswork.

### Why Chicago Homes Need Drain Cleaning

Chicago's older infrastructure, combined with hard water and freezing winters, creates unique drain challenges. Grease buildup from cooking, tree roots seeking moisture in aging clay pipes, and frozen drain lines are common issues we see daily.
