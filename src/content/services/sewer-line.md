## Sewer Line Services in Chicago

Chicago's aging infrastructure means many homes still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines that are decades old. Sandoval Plumbing specializes in diagnosing and repairing sewer line issues.

### Signs of Sewer Line Problems

- Multiple drains backing up at once
- Foul sewage odors in the yard or basement
- Lush patches of grass over the sewer line
- Sinkholes or depressions in the yard
- Gurgling toilets when you run water
- Unexplained rodent or insect activity

### Our Services

**Camera Inspection:** We insert a professional sewer camera to inspect the inside of your line, identifying cracks, tree root intrusion, bellied pipes, and blockages.

**Hydro-Jetting:** High-pressure water cleaning removes grease, scale, and roots from your sewer line.

**Trenchless Repair:** When possible, we use trenchless methods (pipe bursting, CIPP lining) to repair your sewer line without digging up your yard or driveway.

**Excavation & Replacement:** For severely damaged lines, we perform traditional excavation with minimal disruption to your property.

### Chicago's Aging Sewer Lines

Many Chicago neighborhoods still have original clay tile or cast iron sewer lines installed 50-100 years ago. These materials are prone to cracking, root intrusion, and collapse. Regular camera inspections can catch problems early.
