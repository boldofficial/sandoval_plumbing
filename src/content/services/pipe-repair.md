## Pipe Repair & Replacement in Chicago

Chicago homes have pipes made from a variety of materials — copper, galvanized steel, cast iron, and PVC. Age, freezing temperatures, and hard water all take their toll. Sandoval Plumbing repairs and replaces all types of piping.

### Signs You Need Pipe Repair

- Visible rust or corrosion on pipes
- Leaks or water stains on walls/ceilings
- Low water pressure throughout the house
- Discolored water (rusty, brown, or yellow)
- Banging noises in the walls
- Frequent pipe bursts in winter

### Our Pipe Services

**Emergency Pipe Repair:** Burst pipe? We respond within 60 minutes to stop the leak and repair the damage.

**Pipe Replacement:** For old, corroded, or failing pipes, we recommend full replacement with modern PEX or copper piping.

**Frozen Pipe Thawing:** Using safe, non-destructive methods to thaw frozen pipes before they burst.

**Slab Leak Repair:** Leaks in pipes running under concrete slabs — detected and repaired with minimal disruption.

### Chicago's Pipe Challenges

Chicago winters are brutal on pipes. When temperatures drop below freezing, uninsulated pipes can freeze and burst in minutes. We recommend insulating exposed pipes in basements, attics, and crawl spaces.
