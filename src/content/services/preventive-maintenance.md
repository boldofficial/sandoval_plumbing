## Preventive Maintenance in Chicago

Regular plumbing maintenance saves money, prevents emergencies, and extends the life of your plumbing system. Sandoval Plumbing offers comprehensive maintenance plans for Chicago homeowners.

### What We Inspect

**Complete System Inspection:** We check every faucet, toilet, shower, and drain in your home for leaks, slow drainage, and wear.

**Water Heater Check:** Flush the tank, test the pressure relief valve, inspect the anode rod, and check for signs of corrosion.

**Pipe Assessment:** Visual inspection of exposed pipes for leaks, corrosion, and damage.

**Drain Assessment:** Check drain speeds and identify potential clogs before they become problems.

**Sump Pump:** Test operation and backup battery (if applicable).

### Benefits of Preventive Maintenance

- Catch small issues before they become expensive repairs
- Extend the life of your water heater and fixtures
- Lower water bills by fixing hidden leaks
- Peace of mind — no surprise plumbing emergencies
- Priority scheduling if an emergency does occur

### Chicago Seasonal Maintenance

**Spring:** Inspect pipes for winter damage, test sump pump, flush water heater.

**Summer:** Check outdoor faucets and irrigation systems, check for leaks.

**Fall:** Insulate exposed pipes before winter, drain outdoor hoses, service water heater.

**Winter:** Monitor for frozen pipes, check for drafts near pipes, know how to shut off water.
