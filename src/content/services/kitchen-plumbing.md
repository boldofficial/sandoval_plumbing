## Kitchen Plumbing in Chicago

The kitchen is one of the most-used rooms in your home, and plumbing problems here can disrupt your entire daily routine. Sandoval Plumbing provides fast, reliable kitchen plumbing service.

### Kitchen Services

**Faucet Repair & Installation:** Dripping faucets, low water pressure, sprayer issues, and new faucet installation.

**Garbage Disposal:** Installation, repair, and replacement of garbage disposals. We work with InSinkErator, Waste King, and other major brands.

**Dishwasher Hookup:** Installation and repair of dishwasher supply lines and drains.

**Sink:** Undersink leak repair, drain pipe replacement, sink replacement, and trap cleaning.

**Water Filtration:** Undersink water filter installation for cleaner drinking water.

### Common Kitchen Plumbing Issues

- Grease buildup in kitchen drains
- Leaking under the sink
- Garbage disposal jammed or humming
- Low water flow from faucet
- Dishwasher not draining
- Sprayer hose leaking

### Chicago Kitchen Drain Care

Kitchen drains in Chicago homes commonly clog with grease buildup. Avoid pouring grease down the drain, use a drain strainer, and run hot water after each use. For persistent clogs, hydro-jetting is the most effective solution.
