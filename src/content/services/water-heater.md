## Water Heater Services in Chicago

Whether your water heater is leaking, making strange noises, or not producing enough hot water, Sandoval Plumbing can diagnose and fix the problem. We install and repair all major brands.

### Signs You Need Water Heater Service

- No hot water or inconsistent temperature
- Strange noises (popping, rumbling) from the tank
- Rusty or discolored water
- Water pooling around the unit
- Water heater is over 10 years old
- Higher energy bills without explanation

### Our Services

**Tank Water Heaters:** We repair and install traditional tank water heaters from A.O. Smith, Rheem, and Bradford White. Available in 30, 40, 50, and 75+ gallon sizes.

**Tankless Water Heaters:** Energy-efficient tankless systems from Navien and Rinnai provide unlimited hot water on demand. Perfect for Chicago homes with limited space.

**Hybrid Heat Pump:** Energy-efficient hybrid systems that can reduce water heating costs by up to 60%.

### Chicago Hard Water

Chicago has hard water that accelerates mineral buildup in water heaters. We recommend annual flushing and anode rod inspections to extend the life of your unit.
