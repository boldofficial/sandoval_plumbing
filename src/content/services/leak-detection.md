## Leak Detection & Repair in Chicago

Hidden water leaks can cause extensive damage to your home. Sandoval Plumbing uses advanced technology to find leaks quickly and accurately — without guesswork or unnecessary demolition.

### Signs of a Hidden Leak

- Unexplained increase in water bills
- Water stains on ceilings or walls
- Sound of running water when fixtures are off
- Musty odors or mold growth
- Cracks in foundation or walls
- Warm spots on floors (slab leaks)

### Our Detection Methods

**Acoustic Listening:** We use sensitive ground microphones to hear the sound of water escaping from pipes.

**Thermal Imaging:** Infrared cameras detect temperature differences caused by leaking water behind walls and under floors.

**Pressure Testing:** Isolating sections of your plumbing system to pinpoint the leak location.

**Video Camera Inspection:** Small cameras inspect the inside of your pipes to find cracks, breaks, and leaks.

### Why Fast Leak Repair Matters

A small leak can waste 10,000+ gallons of water per year and cause structural damage, mold growth, and higher utility bills. Early detection saves money and prevents major repairs.
